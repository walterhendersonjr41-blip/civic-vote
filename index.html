How this works

Items come straight from official public sources and refresh every 6 hours: Congress (congress.gov), about 270 U.S. cities, counties and local agencies that publish on Legistar, and the UK Parliament. Each card links to the official page.

Votes here are a public opinion poll, not an official vote. They are shared for free over public Nostr relays. There's no account: your phone or computer gets its own random key, so it's one vote per device per item, and you can change your vote anytime.

Not covered yet: state legislatures, and places that don't use Legistar (for example Chattanooga and Hamilton County, TN).
